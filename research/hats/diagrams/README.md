# Sources of the diagrams of "Infinitely Many Hats (3/3)"

The pictures of `content/posts/2020-09-26-infinitely-many-hats-3-3/images/`
were made in 2020 with this Makefile: the automata from the three `.latex`
files, the trees with a `tikz` command of the hats calculator. The
calculator kept in this repository (`figures/hats/hats.js`, the version
published on GitHub) does not have that command, so the trees cannot be
made again from here. The pictures in the post are the ones of 2020, taken
from the WordPress blog.
