**Related work.** The puzzle is recent and, to our knowledge, nothing has
been published on it beyond Jane Street's page [JS22] and the entries of
the OEIS. The up move $n \mapsto \lceil 3n/2 \rceil$ is the map of Mahler's
$Z$-numbers [Ma68] and of Odlyzko and Wilf's work on the Josephus problem
[OW91]; moving up from the odd beads and down from the even ones is the
$3x+1$ map. The rosary differs from both in that Jane chooses her moves.
These relatives are discussed in the coda, once the vocabulary is in place.
