## Declaration on the use of AI tools

DRAFT, TO BE REVIEWED AND REWORDED BY THE AUTHOR BEFORE ANY SUBMISSION.

The 2023 version of this paper (shortest recitations, the reluctant
recitation never halts, the onyx rosary) was written by the author alone.
The present version was prepared in November 2026 with the assistance of
Claude, an AI model made by Anthropic, used as a research and writing
assistant: it proposed the proofs of Theorems 3, 5 and 6, wrote the
programs that test the statements and the Lean formalization, and drafted
most of the new text. The author directed the work and takes
responsibility for the content.

The statements listed in the section "Checked by a proof assistant" do not
rest on trust in either the author or the assistant: their proofs are
checked by Lean. The other statements, and the correspondence between the
Lean statements and the text, rest on the usual reading.
