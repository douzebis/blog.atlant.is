# The game of hats: history

What was done to the article, version by version. The reader-level
summary shown on the site is the `history` list in the article's front
matter; this file is the detailed account. Earlier versions are kept,
unmodified, under `content/versions/the-game-of-hats/` and listed with
their digests in `docs/papers/frozen-versions.sha256`.

## 2020 — three posts

- "Towers of hats (1/3)" (2020-08-30), "Hats Calculator (2/3)"
  (2020-09-05) and "Infinitely Many Hats (3/3)" (2020-09-26), published
  on the WordPress blog `blog.douzeb.is`.
  In 2020 the blog was at `blog.atlant.is`. Dated traces of the posts
  that do not depend on this repository (checked 2026-11-28 against the
  frozen copies, sentence by sentence):

  - Internet Archive, 28 September 2020, two days after the third post:
    <https://web.archive.org/web/20200928125658/https://blog.atlant.is/?cat=16>
    has the text of all three posts (206 of 207 plain sentences of the
    first, 48 of 49 of the third, and the prose of the second). This is
    the capture to cite.
  - Same day, <https://web.archive.org/web/20200928110447/https://blog.atlant.is/?paged=2&author=1>:
    the first and second posts.
  - Captures of single posts, later: the third post on 21 June 2021,
    <https://web.archive.org/web/20210621232806/https://blog.atlant.is/?p=2817>;
    the first on 2 December 2022,
    <https://web.archive.org/web/20221202084527/https://blog.atlant.is/?p=1183>.
    The post numbers are 1183, 1893 and 2817.
  - The public repository `github.com/douzebis/blog.atlant.is-import`,
    created on 16 September 2020 according to GitHub, has the three
    posts with commits of September 2020. The dates are those of the
  posts; the dates of their last edits are not known. The calculator
  that they link to was last changed on 2022-11-11
  (`github.com/douzebis/hats`, commit a7d108a).
- Imported on 2026-11-24 as they stood on the WordPress blog (post 1
  from the Markdown export, posts 2 and 3 from the pages served, because
  the export is truncated), with the pictures of *Team Fortress 2*
  credited to Valve in their captions.
- Kept on 2026-11-27 as one earlier version in three parts,
  `content/versions/the-game-of-hats/2020/`, one directory per post
  under the name it had among the posts, so that the links from one
  part to another hold. Only the lines `version`, `version_date`,
  `version_of` and `version_note` were added to their front matter.
- In these texts the players point at white hats, white is the digit 1,
  a stack of hats is a "tower", and the resets and shifts are "left" and
  "right". What is wrong with them is listed in
  `docs/specs/0005-hats-articles-revision.md`.
- They link to the calculator of the site, which changes with the
  article (colors, names): what a reader of the kept posts sees there is
  today's calculator (a limit of `docs/invariants/0003`).
